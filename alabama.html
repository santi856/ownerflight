Inside the Alabama dataset

OwnerFlight tracks 120 home care agencies across Alabama. What the verification pass has established so far:

  • No license numbers — by design of the state, not the data

    Alabama does not license non-medical home care, so the license field is empty on all 120 records. That is structural, not a gap in research: there is no state registry to match against.

  • Owners are named on 15 records

    An owner or administrator is identified on 15 of the 120 agencies. Four records carry a verified, published email address — the contact layer the DFY team works from; verified emails are not part of the DIY database.

  • Booking is almost entirely by phone

    Of the 12 agencies whose booking status has been verified, 11 take no online booking at all. The single exception is homeRN in Tuscaloosa.

The full record for each agency — owner or administrator where published, phone, website, Google rating and review count, booking and social signals, and a 0–100 priority score with the reason attached — lives in the subscriber database.
