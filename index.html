Questions

Before you ask.

Who is this for?

Any business that sells to home care agency owners — software, staffing, billing, consulting, franchising, senior placement, and the agencies' own vendors. If your buyers are agency owners, this is your front door to them.

What exactly happens after I pay?

You tell us your market and what you sell. We confirm the exact owner count for that market in writing, draft the outreach angle for your approval, then run the flight. Replies land in real conversations; interested owners get booked on your calendar with their name, agency, and what they said.

Which channels do you use?

All of them: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, email, and text — matched to how each owner actually responds. Outreach sends from our accounts, never your domain or your phone number.

What counts as a positive reply?

An owner showing any interest in talking — asking a question, asking for information, agreeing to a call. Wrong numbers, gatekeepers, and clear no's don't count, and you'll see every reply either way in your weekly report.

Why only 10 deal spots?

Every flight is worked by people, reply by reply. Ten deals is what we can run properly at once. When the 10 are gone, the Deal is gone — the standing way in is DFY at $1,500/mo.

Can I see the data first?

Yes — the sample page shows the record format, and the exact owner count for your market is confirmed in writing before anything sends.
