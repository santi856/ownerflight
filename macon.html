Methodology

Drawn from the OwnerFlight Georgia database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Every Macon statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated. Georgia's Department of Community Health publishes no machine-readable license roster, so this file was assembled from directories and agency websites; a license number is recorded on 1 of the 20 Macon records. Online booking has been classified on only 2 of Georgia's 225 records, so no booking statistic is published for Macon.
