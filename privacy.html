Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What we collect from subscribers and partners

  • Your name, business name, and email address — so we can deliver the product, send service emails, and attribute partner referrals.
  • Payment is processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Emails you send us, including replies to outreach, so we can work your request or, for DFY clients, your campaign replies.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share subscriber or partner information with third parties except the processors required to run the service (such as Stripe for payments and our email provider for delivery).
  • This website does not run advertising trackers.

How this site measures visits

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how this website is used — pages visited, approximate region, and device type, counted in aggregate. Analytics helps us fix pages nobody can find and keep the ones people actually use. It is not advertising tracking: this site runs no ad trackers, sells no visitor data, and does not build profiles of individual visitors. If you email us or buy from us, that information is handled under the sections above — it is never mixed into advertising audiences, because there aren't any. You can also block analytics cookies in your browser settings and every other part of this site will keep working exactly the same way.

How long we keep information

Subscriber and partner records are kept while the relationship is active and for a reasonable period afterward for billing, tax, and dispute records. Outreach removal requests are kept indefinitely — that is what makes a removal permanent rather than temporary. Visit analytics are held by Google Analytics on its standard retention settings.

About the agency database

The database is compiled by our research team from state licensing data and business information agencies publish themselves (their own websites and public listings). It covers businesses, not consumers. If you represent an agency in the database and want a record corrected or removed, email us and we will take care of it.

Your choices

  • Reply to any email from us and you will be removed from outreach — permanently.
  • Ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or to delete it, at the address below. Deleting your subscriber information ends your subscription delivery.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and note the new date above. Material changes are also emailed to subscribers.

Privacy — questions, answered

Do you sell my personal information?

No. We do not sell subscriber, partner, or visitor information — not to data brokers, not to advertisers, not to other agencies. The only third parties that touch it are the processors required to run the service, such as Stripe for payments and our email provider for delivery.

Does this website track me?

It counts visits in aggregate with Google Analytics 4 — which pages get used, in roughly which region, on what kind of device. It does not run advertising trackers, and we do not build profiles of individual visitors.

How do I get my agency removed from the database?

Email jesusasantiago413@gmail.com from an address that identifies your agency and ask for a correction or removal. The database covers businesses, compiled from state licensing data and information agencies publish themselves, and we act on removal requests directly.

How do I stop outreach emails?

Reply to any email from us and you will be removed from outreach permanently. You can also ask at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at the same address.
